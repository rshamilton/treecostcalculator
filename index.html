Tree Cost Calculator

Tree Cost Calculator is a free iPhone and iPad app for tree service professionals that turns a tree's circumference and height into a priced tree removal estimate, then sends it to the customer as a PDF estimate or invoice. No ads, no account, open source.

What can Tree Cost Calculator do?

Everything a tree service needs to go from a tape measure to a paid invoice, on one phone.

Estimate

  • Price each tree from trunk circumference (or diameter) and height, in feet or meters
  • Unlimited trees per job, each with a name, measurements and photos
  • Starting fee, plus a rate per cubic foot or cubic meter
  • Name each estimate, such as “Backyard oak removal”; search and rename from the list
  • Customer details, job site address and notes
  • Import a customer from Contacts; tap to call, email or get directions

Price any job

  • Other Work line items: quantity × unit price with an optional minimum charge
  • Stump grinding priced per inch of diameter, trimming and pruning, haul away, crew labor per hour, crane or bucket truck, emergency storm work
  • A saved price list so you enter each price once
  • Additional charge and discount, as dollars or percent
  • Tax regions: a tax rate and license number for each state you work in
  • Deposit due on acceptance

Get paid

  • Professional PDF estimate with logo, license number, terms, photos and signature block
  • Customer signs on your phone; the signature prints on the PDF
  • One-tap convert estimate to invoice
  • Invoiced and Paid status, payment due date, amount paid, balance due and payment instructions
  • Statuses Draft, Sent, Approved, Declined, Invoiced and Paid with running totals for Open, Won and Unpaid

Trunk diameter and stumps

  • Enter a tree by trunk diameter in inches or cm; the app converts it to circumference (π × diameter)
  • Each tree shows its approximate diameter
  • “Stump grinding for…” adds a stump grinding line sized from that tree’s diameter
  • Priced from your saved stump grinding service: a price per inch with an optional minimum charge

Your data

  • No ads, no account, no analytics; works offline
  • Saved as ordinary files in a TCC folder in the Files app
  • Optional backup and restore with your own iCloud Drive
  • CSV export of all estimates; search and duplicate
  • Dark mode, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver
  • Open source under GNU GPL v3

How is the price of a tree removal worked out?

The app prices each tree from its estimated volume: price = starting fee + volume × your rate, where the volume is the trunk treated as a cylinder.

volume = circumference × circumference × height ÷ 12.56

The number 12.56 is 4π (circumference is 2π times the radius, so this equals π × radius² × height). In feet the volume is in cubic feet; in meters it is cubic meters. You set the starting fee and the rate; the app never assumes market prices.

Worked example

StepCalculationResult
Trunk circumference6 ft6 ft
Height50 ft50 ft
Volume6 × 6 × 50 ÷ 12.56143.3 cu ft
Volume price143.3121 × $10 per cu ft$1,433.12
Starting fee$150$150.00
Tree price$1,433.12 + $150.00$1,583.12

The $10 rate and $150 fee are example numbers only. Additional charges, discounts, line items, tax and a deposit are then applied on top. Try your own numbers in the free tree removal cost calculator.

Who is Tree Cost Calculator for?

It is built for people who price and invoice tree work:

  • Arborists. Price removals and keep photos and measurements with every job.
  • Tree removal and trimming companies. Send a written estimate from the driveway, get it signed on the phone, invoice when the work is done.
  • Stump grinding services. Price stumps by the inch with a minimum charge, several stumps on one job.
  • Landscapers. Add tree work to a job with line items, a discount and your local tax rate.
  • Homeowners. Check a quote: measure the trunk and height, then see how a volume-based price compares with the number you were given.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tree Cost Calculator free?

Yes. Tree Cost Calculator is completely free: no ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases and no account. It is open source under the GNU GPL v3, so anyone can read the code.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

No account is needed and the app works offline. You can measure, price, sign and create PDF estimates and invoices with no signal at all. An internet connection is only used by features of iOS you choose, such as emailing a PDF or iCloud Drive backup.

Where is my data stored?

On your own device, as ordinary files in a folder named TCC that you can open in the Files app. If you leave it on, the app also keeps a backup copy in your own iCloud Drive so your estimates, photos and settings come back after a reinstall or on a new iPhone or iPad. The developer receives no data and uses no analytics.

Can I make invoices?

Yes. Convert any estimate to an invoice with one tap. An invoice has Invoiced and Paid statuses, a payment due date, the amount paid, the balance due and your payment instructions, and it exports as a PDF just like an estimate.

Can the customer sign on my phone?

Yes. The customer signs on the screen of your iPhone or iPad. The signature is saved with the estimate and printed in the signature block of the PDF. Whether a signed estimate is legally binding depends on where you work, so check with a local attorney.

How do I price stump grinding?

On a tree, tap “Stump grinding for…” to add a stump grinding line sized from that tree’s diameter, priced from your saved stump grinding service. You can also add an Other Work line item by hand: quantity is the diameter in inches, the unit price is your rate per inch, and you can set a minimum charge. The price is diameter × price per inch, or the minimum if that is higher. Saved prices are kept in a price list so you only enter them once. You can also try it in the free stump grinding cost calculator.

Can I enter the trunk diameter instead of the circumference?

Yes. You can enter a tree by trunk diameter: type the trunk diameter in inches or centimeters. The app converts it to circumference (circumference = π × diameter) and prices the tree the same way, and each tree shows its approximate diameter. You can still enter circumference if you prefer. Measure at about 4½ feet (1.4 m) above the ground.

Can I name my estimates?

Yes. Give any estimate an optional job name, such as “Backyard oak removal”. It appears in the estimate list, is searchable, and is printed on the PDF. If you leave it blank, the customer’s name is used. To rename an estimate, do it from the list.

Does it work in meters?

Yes. Choose feet or meters. Volume is then in cubic feet or cubic meters and your rate is per cu ft or per m³. Each estimate keeps the unit it was created with.

Is Tree Cost Calculator open source?

Yes. The full source code is published at github.com/rshamilton/tcc-app under the GNU General Public License v3 (GPL v3). You can read it, build it, report bugs and suggest features there.

Can I use it on iPad?

Yes. It runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later). On iPad your estimates sit in a sidebar beside the open estimate, and the app works in Split View and Slide Over.
