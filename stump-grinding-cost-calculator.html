How do you price stump grinding?

Stump grinding is usually priced by size: price = stump diameter in inches × your price per inch, with a minimum charge so small stumps still cover your travel and setup. The price per inch and the minimum are yours to set; this page does not claim any market price.

price = max(diameter × price per inch, minimum charge)

Where do I measure the stump diameter?

Measure straight across the widest point of the stump, in inches. If the stump has a flared root base, decide on a convention (across the cut top, or across the flare at ground level) and use it every time, so your prices stay consistent. If you only have the circumference, divide by π (3.14159) to get the diameter, as in the trunk diameter calculator.

What else affects a stump grinding price?

Diameter is only a starting point. Depth to grind, large surface roots, rocks, slope and access for the machine, and cleanup or haul-away of the chips all change the work. Price those as extra line items or adjust your rate. The calculator above applies the minimum to each stump separately.

What does this calculator leave out?

It has no tax, travel or cleanup charges, and its results are estimates for planning, not a quote. Check every figure before giving a price to a customer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate stump grinding cost?

Multiply the stump's diameter in inches by your price per inch, and charge your minimum if that total is lower. For example, 24 in × $3 = $72, so a $100 minimum applies (example numbers).

How do I measure a stump for grinding?

Measure across the widest point of the stump in inches. If you only know the circumference, divide it by 3.14159 to get the diameter. Pick one convention for flared root bases and use it consistently.

Does the minimum charge apply to each stump?

In this calculator, yes: each stump is charged the larger of its size-based price and the minimum. In the app, the minimum charge belongs to each line item.
