How big is a cord of firewood?

A full cord is a stack of wood 4 ft high, 4 ft deep and 8 ft long, which is 128 cubic feet (about 3.62 m³) of stacked wood including the air between the pieces.

cords = length × height × depth (in feet) ÷ 128

What is a face cord?

A face cord (also called a rick or a rack, depending on the region) is a stack 4 ft high and 8 ft long with pieces cut shorter than 4 ft, usually 16 inches. A 16-inch face cord is 4 × 8 × 1.333 = 42.67 cubic feet, which is one third of a full cord. The term is not standardized, so always confirm the length of the pieces. This calculator reports face cords as one third of a full cord, which matches the 16-inch definition.

What is a worked example?

A stack 96 in (8 ft) long, 48 in (4 ft) high and 16 in deep is 8 × 4 × 1.333 = 42.67 cu ft. Divided by 128 that is 0.333 cord, exactly one 16-inch face cord (1.0 face cord). At an example price of $300 per cord, that stack is $100.

How do I measure a stack of firewood?

Measure the length along the face, the height of the face at its lowest point, and the depth, which is the length of the pieces. Tight, straight stacks measure true; a loose or crooked pile holds more air and less wood than the same measurement suggests.

What are the limits?

A cord is a volume of stacked wood, not solid wood and not a weight. Results are estimates, and local rules about selling firewood vary, so check yours.

Frequently asked questions

How many cubic feet are in a cord of firewood?

128 cubic feet of stacked wood: 4 ft high × 4 ft deep × 8 ft long.

How much is a face cord compared with a full cord?

A face cord of 16-inch pieces (4 ft × 8 ft × 16 in) is 42.67 cubic feet, one third of a full cord. The term varies by region, so confirm the piece length.

How do I convert a stack to cords?

Multiply length × height × depth in feet and divide by 128. Enter the three measurements above and the calculator does it for you.
