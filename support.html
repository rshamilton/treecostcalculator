Support

Help with Tree Cost Calculator: getting started, common questions and how to reach us.

Contact us

Email treecostcalculator@googlegroups.com and include your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and the app version (Settings → Version in the app). Screenshots help. We usually reply within two business days.

Report a bug or suggest a feature

Tree Cost Calculator is open source. The fastest way to report a bug or suggest a feature is to open an issue on GitHub: github.com/rshamilton/tcc-app/issues. Please do not include customer details or other private information in a public issue. You can also email us.

Getting started

  1. Set your prices. The first time you open the app, enter your starting fee, price per cubic foot (or cubic meter) and tax rate. You can change them later in Settings.
  2. Create an estimate. Tap New Estimate, then enter the tree's circumference and height. The volume and price update as you type.
  3. Add more trees. Tap Add Another Tree for each additional tree on the job. Use the … menu on a tree to duplicate or delete it.
  4. Add other work. Add line items such as stump grinding, trimming, haul away or crew labor from your price list.
  5. Add the customer, photos and notes. Fill in the customer's details (or import them from Contacts), attach photos of each tree or the site, and add any notes.
  6. Adjust the price. Add an additional charge or a discount, change the tax rate or pick a saved tax region, and set a deposit, for this job only.
  7. Send it. Tap Preview & Share to review the PDF, have the customer sign on your phone if you like, then share it by email, message or anything else on your phone.
  8. Invoice it. When the job is done, convert the estimate to an invoice with one tap and mark it Paid when you are paid.

What's in the app

  • Live pricing from circumference and height, in feet or meters.
  • Multiple trees per estimate, each with its own name, measurements and photos.
  • Other Work line items (stump grinding per inch, trimming, haul away, crew labor, crane or bucket truck, storm work) with a saved price list and optional minimum charge.
  • Starting fee, additional charge, discount, tax and deposit, as a fixed amount or a percentage.
  • Professional PDF with your logo, license number, contact details, itemized trees, photos, terms and a signature block.
  • Customer signature captured on the phone and printed on the PDF.
  • Invoices with Invoiced and Paid status, due date, amount paid, balance due and payment instructions.
  • Status tracking (Draft, Sent, Approved, Declined, Invoiced, Paid) with running totals for Open, Won and Unpaid.
  • Contacts, call, email and directions from the customer details.
  • Tree Tools: tree height, trunk diameter, stump grinding price, firewood cords, log board feet and approximate log weight.
  • Search, duplicate, delete and CSV export of all estimates.
  • Work in more than one state. Save a tax rate (and optional license number) for each state or area.
  • iPhone and iPad. On iPad your estimates sit in a sidebar beside the open estimate, and the app works in Split View and Slide Over.
  • Private, offline and backed up. Everything is saved as files on your device (visible in the Files app), with an optional copy in your own iCloud Drive. The app works without a connection.

Common questions

How is the volume calculated?

The app treats the trunk as a cylinder: volume = circumference × circumference × height ÷ 12.56. (Circumference is 2π times the radius, so this equals π × radius² × height.) With feet the result is cubic feet; with meters it is cubic meters. Your price is then starting fee + volume × your rate. Try it in the tree removal cost calculator.

How do I measure a tree?

Wrap a tape around the trunk at about 4½ feet (1.4 m) above the ground for the circumference, and measure the tree's total height from the ground to the top, or use the Tree Height tool. Enter both in the same unit.

How do I price stump grinding?

Add an Other Work line, choose stump grinding (priced per inch), enter the stump diameter in inches as the quantity, and set your price per inch and an optional minimum charge. The line price is quantity × unit price, or the minimum if that is higher. Prices you use are saved in your price list. See also the stump grinding cost calculator.

What are Other Work line items?

Line items for anything that is not priced by tree volume: stump grinding, trimming and pruning, haul away, crew labor per hour, crane or bucket truck, emergency storm work, or anything you add yourself. Each line is quantity × unit price with an optional minimum charge. Your saved price list fills in your usual prices.

How do I turn an estimate into an invoice?

Open the estimate and choose to convert it to an invoice. The status changes to Invoiced; you can then set the payment due date, record the amount paid and see the balance due. Mark it Paid when you have been paid. Your payment instructions (set in Settings) print on the invoice PDF.

Can customers sign the estimate on my phone?

Yes. Hand the phone or iPad to the customer and have them sign with a finger or Apple Pencil. The signature is saved with the estimate (stored like a photo) and printed in the signature block of the PDF. Whether a signed estimate is binding, and what wording or notices it needs, depends on where you work and the job. Put any wording required in your area under Settings → Estimate PDF → Terms, and consider having a local attorney review it.

Can I change my prices for one job?

Yes. Every estimate starts from your defaults in Settings, and you can change the starting fee, price per unit, additional charge, discount and tax on the estimate itself. Changing Settings later does not alter existing estimates.

What if I switch between feet and meters?

The unit you choose in Settings applies to new estimates. Existing estimates keep the unit they were created with, so their numbers never change unexpectedly.

How do I put my logo on the PDF?

Open Settings → Your Business, tap Add Logo and choose an image. Add your company name, phone, email and address there too.

I work in more than one state. How do I handle different tax rates?

Open Settings → Tax Regions and add each state or area with its tax rate and, if you like, its license number. Then, on an estimate, choose the region under Tax region in the Pricing section and the tax rate (and license number) switch to match. The PDF shows the region name on the tax line. You are responsible for entering the correct rates for the places you work.

Where does my license number appear?

Add it under Settings → Your Business and it prints under your contact details on every estimate. If you work in several states, give each tax region its own license number; it replaces the main one on estimates that use that region.

How do I add a customer from Contacts?

On an estimate, choose to add the customer from Contacts and pick one contact in the system contact picker. The app receives only the contact you pick, and does not ask for access to your whole address book. Once a customer is saved you can tap their phone number or email to call or write, and their address to get directions.

How do I export my estimates to a spreadsheet?

Use the CSV export in the app to save or share a file with all your estimates. It opens in Numbers, Excel and Google Sheets.

How do the Tree Tools work, and how accurate are they?

Tree Tools has a height measurement using the tilt sensor, trunk diameter (DBH) from circumference, a stump grinding price, firewood cords (full and face cord), log board feet (Doyle and International ¼-inch rules) and approximate green log weight by species. They are estimates for planning and pricing, not surveyed measurements. Lean, slope and hard-to-see treetops all affect height readings. See the tree height calculator and firewood cord calculator for the formulas.

Can I edit an estimate after sending it?

Yes. Open it from the list, make your changes and share it again. Use the status menu at the top of the estimate to mark it Sent, Approved, Declined, Invoiced or Paid.

How do I share or print an estimate?

Tap Preview & Share at the bottom of the estimate, then use the share button. The share sheet includes Mail, Messages, Save to Files and Print.

Where is my data stored? Will I lose it if I delete the app?

Everything is saved as ordinary files in a folder called TCC on your device (TCC is the app's name on your Home Screen): open the Files app and look under On My iPhone (or On My iPad). Get Info on the folder to see how much space it uses; inside are Estimates, Photos, signatures, your logo and your settings. If iCloud Drive is on, the app also keeps a copy in your own iCloud Drive, so deleting and reinstalling the app, or installing it on a new iPhone or iPad, brings your estimates, photos and settings back automatically. If something is missing (for example because iCloud was still downloading), use Settings → Your Data → Restore from iCloud. If iCloud is off or unavailable, deleting the app deletes the data, so copy the folder somewhere safe first. You can turn the iCloud copy on or off under Settings → Your Data. The developer never receives your data and can't restore it for you.

How do I remove the iCloud copy?

Deleting the app does not delete the copy in iCloud Drive. Open the Files app, choose iCloud Drive, and delete the TCC folder. You can also go to Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

How do I delete an estimate?

Swipe it left in the list and confirm, or open it and choose Delete Estimate from the status menu. Its photos are removed too.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app needs no internet connection. (Only the Support and Privacy links in Settings need one.)

Troubleshooting

Photos are missing from the PDF

Check that Include photos in the PDF is on in Settings → Estimate PDF, and that the photos appear on the estimate itself.

The totals don't match my calculation

Check the price per unit, starting fee, line items, tax rate, discount and additional charge on the estimate. Percentages apply in this order: additional charge, then discount, then tax on the discounted amount. Each line is rounded to the nearest cent.

The camera isn't working

Go to iOS Settings → Tree Cost Calculator and make sure Camera is allowed. You can also choose photos from your library instead.

The height reading looks wrong

Hold the phone steady, stand on level ground at a measured distance, and aim at the true top of the tree. Leaning trees, slopes and tops hidden in the canopy all reduce accuracy. Treat the result as an estimate.

I can't find an old estimate

Clear the search box and the status filter at the top of the list. Estimates live on the device where they were created, and in your iCloud Drive copy if that is turned on (Settings → Your Data → Restore from iCloud).
