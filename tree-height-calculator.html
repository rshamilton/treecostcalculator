How do you measure tree height with a phone?

Use the tangent method. Stand a measured distance from the trunk on level ground, aim your phone's tilt sensor (a clinometer) at the top of the tree and read the angle above level. Then:

height = distance × tan(angle to top) + eye height

If the base of the tree is below your eye level, measure the angle down to the base too and use height = distance × (tan(top angle) + tan(base angle)).

What is a worked example?

Standing 50 ft from the trunk with your eye at 5.5 ft, and reading 55° to the top: tan(55°) = 1.428, so 50 × 1.428 = 71.4 ft, plus 5.5 ft eye height = 76.9 ft.

How do I get the most accurate reading?

  • Measure the distance to the trunk with a tape, not by eye or by pacing.
  • Stand where you can see the true top, often about as far from the tree as it is tall; very steep angles amplify small errors.
  • Hold the phone steady and read the angle at the highest point you can truly identify.
  • On a slope, measure the base angle as well (or measure from level ground).
  • A leaning tree should be measured from a spot at right angles to the lean.

How do I find the diameter of a tree from its circumference?

Divide the circumference by π: diameter = circumference ÷ 3.14159. Foresters call the diameter measured about 4½ ft (1.4 m) above the ground the DBH (diameter at breast height). Example: a 37.7 in circumference gives 37.7 ÷ 3.14159 = 12.0 in.

What are the limits of these calculators?

Height from an angle is only as good as the distance and angle you enter. Phone sensors, treetops hidden in the canopy, leaning trunks and slopes all add error, so treat the result as an estimate. Never stand where a falling limb or tree could hurt you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the height of a tree?

Stand a measured distance from the tree on level ground, measure the angle to the top, and compute height = distance × tan(angle) + your eye height. For 50 ft, 55° and 5.5 ft eye height that is about 76.9 ft.

Can a phone measure tree height?

Yes, approximately. A phone's tilt sensor can act as a clinometer to read the angle to the top of the tree; combine it with a measured distance using the tangent formula. Expect estimates, not survey-grade results.

How do I find tree diameter from circumference?

Divide the circumference by π (3.14159). A 37.7 inch circumference is about a 12.0 inch diameter.
